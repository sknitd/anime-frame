Prompt tips (from Decart's docs)
  • Describe the look with concrete visual details: medium, line work, colours, lighting.
  • Standard model: short style phrases work, e.g. "Studio Ghibli animation style" or "Cyberpunk city".
  • ⚡ Fast model: describe a whole-scene change, e.g. "Transform the entire scene into a watercolor painting". Leave out studio, brand and character names here; they're refused as copyrighted.
  • One idea per prompt. Skip filler like "realistic" or "seamless", and name things by what they look like, not "it" or "this".
  • Up to about 750 characters. Keep Enhance on so Decart expands short prompts; turn it off for exact wording.
  • Prompts apply live, without reconnecting.
